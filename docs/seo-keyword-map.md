## 1. Search-Intent Clustering Principles

1. **One Principal Intent Per URL:** No two URLs may target the same primary commercial keyword or operational problem.
2. **Commercial vs. Recruitment Isolation:** Driver job search terms are strictly confined to `/drivers/*` and never mixed with commercial B2B transport terms.
3. **Evidence Distinction:**
   - `[CONFIRMED]`: Verified commercial query actively targeted in the UK market by established providers.
   - `[STRATEGIC HYPOTHESIS]`: High-probability growth term derived from market expansion and technology differentiation.
